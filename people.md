<div class="person__text">
<p>Martin De Kauwe is Professor of Global Change Ecology and Deputy Head of the School of Biological Sciences at the <a href="http://www.bristol.ac.uk/biology/">University of Bristol</a>.</p>
<p>Martin previously worked at the University of New South Wales, Macquarie University, and the UK Centre for Ecology &amp; Hydrology, and completed his PhD at University College London.</p>
<p>Martin is a member of the Scientific Steering Committee for the <a href="https://amazonface.unicamp.br/">AmazonFACE</a> experiment and part of the senior management team for <a href="https://jules.jchmr.org/">JULES</a> (the UK land surface model) under the Joint Land Modelling Programme (JLMP). Martin previously co-chaired the leadership committee for <a href="https://github.com/CABLE-LSM/CABLE">CABLE</a>, Australia's land surface model. Finally, Martin is an Editor at <a href="https://nph.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/journal/14698137">New Phytologist</a>.</p>
